# Voiceover script, version 3

Version 3 changes four lines, marked **re-record (v3)**: p2, p4, p6 and p13. The other eleven stay as they are.

For ElevenLabs. Fifteen lines, one audio file each, so every cut can be timed on its own. Export each as MP3 named as
shown (`p1.mp3` … `p15.mp3`) and drop them in `video/assets/audio/`, replacing the old ones.

**Voice:** the same voice and settings as before. Calm and plain, mid pace, small pauses at full stops, no upward lift
at the end of sentences.

**Suggested settings:** Stability 45–55, Similarity 75, Style 0–10, Speed 1.0.

**Say it like this:** Nomi = NO-mee. Raita = RYE-tah. Atta = UH-taa. SAM is said as a word, "Sam". Muse = MYOOZ.
Nightshift is one word.

The `<break>` tags are ElevenLabs pauses; keep them in the text you paste. The whole script runs about 2 minutes 45 seconds
at speed 1.0, which leaves room for the cuts and the end card inside 3 minutes.

**How it's written:** short sentences, everyday words, and only claims the app shows on screen. Every number is from the
live run in the recordings (13 dishes, 2 to check, 8 rules, fried onions missing, 3 of 24 plates, 2 oil tins, 2 atta
bags, minimum 3).

---

### p1 · The problem · about 17 s

A head chef plans a dish once. <break time="0.4s" /> Then other cooks make it hundreds of times. <break time="0.5s" /> Over the weeks, the lemon goes missing, and the fried onions get left off. <break time="0.6s" /> Guests notice, but the few who say so write in days later, <break time="0.2s" /> and nobody knows which plate they got.

### p2 · Nomi · about 8 s · **re-record (v3)**

Presenting Nomi. <break time="0.5s" /> It keeps every plate the way the chef planned it, using six Meta models. <break time="0.4s" /> What follows is the live app.

### p3 · Menu · about 10 s

Setup starts with a photo of the menu. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Wonder reads all thirteen dishes, and flags two it couldn't read. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Spark lists what goes on each plate.

### p4 · Golden Plate · about 15 s · **re-record (v3)**

Next, the chef takes three photos of the finished dish. <break time="0.3s" /> SAM three point one finds each part, from the rice to the raita, <break time="0.2s" /> and keeps a part only if it's in all three. <break time="0.4s" /> That's the Golden Plate. <break time="0.3s" /> When the cooks change, the dish stays the same.

### p5 · Rules · about 11 s

The chef says the plating rules out loud, in Hindi. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Voice writes them down, Muse Spark turns them into eight checks, <break time="0.2s" /> and Muse Image draws the card the cooks follow.

### p6 · The pass · about 19 s · **re-record (v3)**

During service, a camera at the pass checks each plate. <break time="0.3s" /> Glimmer is built to run on the restaurant's own computer, keeping only frames with a plate, so faces stay in the kitchen. <break time="0.4s" /> SAM three point one compares the plate with the Golden Plate. <break time="0.4s" /> The fried onions are missing, <break time="0.2s" /> so the cook gets a reminder before it goes out.

### p7 · The guest · about 16 s

At the table, the guest scans a QR code and photographs their plate. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Spark names the dish. <break time="0.4s" /> Then they just talk, in any language. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Voice writes it down, Muse Spark turns it into tags, <break time="0.2s" /> and the guest can fix them before staff see them.

### p8 · The manager · about 10 s

The manager sees the note beside the guest's photo. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Spark drafts a reply in the guest's own Hinglish, <break time="0.2s" /> and nothing is sent until the manager approves it.

### p9 · Nightshift · about 15 s

After closing, the owner runs Nightshift. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Wonder plans the review, and four Muse Spark agents check notes, plates, stock and the closing round at once. <break time="0.4s" /> The kitchen gets a watch list of counts, <break time="0.2s" /> like mint chutney missing on three of twenty-four plates.

### p10 · Stock · about 14 s

Stock works the same way. <break time="0.3s" /> From a shelf photo, SAM three point one puts a box on each item: <break time="0.2s" /> two oil tins, and two bags of atta. <break time="0.4s" /> Two tins is under the minimum of three, so oil goes on a draft order for the manager.

### p11 · Handover · about 9 s

At the end of a shift, the team records a spoken handover. <break time="0.3s" /> Muse Voice writes it down, and Muse Spark sorts it into done, pending and problems.

### p12 · Plating cards · about 6 s

Each cook reads the plating cards in their own language. <break time="0.2s" /> Here, Muse Spark translates one into Kannada.

### p13 · Ask Nomi · about 8 s · **re-record (v3)**

Asked how much biryani will sell on Saturday, <break time="0.2s" /> Ask Nomi says it can't forecast that, <break time="0.2s" /> and shows how many guests picked biryani each day this week.

### p14 · The model log · about 6 s

The app also lists every model call it makes, with the model and how long it took.

### p15 · Close · about 10 s

Nomi reports only what it can see, count or hear, <break time="0.3s" /> and a person approves every reply and every order. <break time="0.7s" /> Every plate, the way the chef meant it.

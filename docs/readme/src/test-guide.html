Judges: test the Golden Plate in two minutes.

1

Show a plate on a laptop

Open demo-assets/golden-plates/chicken-biryani.jpg on GitHub and click it to view it full size.

2

Open Nomi on your phone

Web: bit.ly/try-nomi · Android APK: bit.ly/try-nomi-apk. Sign in with any 10-digit number and the code shown on screen.

3

Pick Kitchen staff → Check my plate

Choose Chicken Biryani, hold the phone over the laptop as if it were the plate, and take the photo.

4

Read the verdict, then break it

"Ready to go". Then show the plate in off-standard/ with the onions missing: "Fix before it goes".

Screen glare can trip the photo check; tilt the laptop a little.

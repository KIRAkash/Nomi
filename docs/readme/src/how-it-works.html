One plate, four moments.

The chef's own photo of the dish done right is the standard. Everything after it is measured against that, never against a guess.

1

Set the Golden Plate

Head chef · once per dish

Three photos of the dish plated right. SAM 3.1 finds every part in all three, then the chef says the rules out loud.

2

Check it at the pass

Pass tablet · every plate

A camera over the pass compares each plate with the Golden Plate before it leaves. Missing onions get a quiet nudge, not an alarm.

3

Hear the guest

Table QR · about ten seconds

No app, no sign-in. Show the plate, tap a face, say anything in any language. Nomi reads back what it heard.

4

Brief the owner

NIGHTSHIFT · every morning

Analysts read the night's facts and write a briefing with evidence, plus a watch list for tonight's service.

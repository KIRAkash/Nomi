A head chef designs a dish once. Then other cooks make it hundreds of times, across shifts, new hires and busy nights. The plate drifts. Guests notice. Few say so, and no one can tell which plate they meant.

Set the standard once. Three photos of the chef's own plate. SAM 3.1 finds every part.

A part counts only if it is found in all three photos, so the Golden Plate is something the kitchen can repeat, not one lucky shot. Anything Nomi is unsure of stays amber until the chef checks it.

    Then the chef says the rules out loud.

    Muse Voice writes down what the chef said, in Hindi or English. Muse Spark turns it into checks the pass can measure. Muse Image draws the plating card every cook follows.

    At the pass, a camera checks every plate. Only frames with a plate go out. Faces never leave the kitchen. Fried onions: not found. A quiet nudge, before it leaves.

    Glimmer is built to run on the restaurant's own computer and keeps only the frames that show a plate. SAM 3.1 compares the plate with the Golden Plate and names the part that is missing, quietly.

    Every morning, NIGHTSHIFT reads the night. One plan. Four analysts, in parallel. Tonight's watch list.

    Muse Wonder plans a review of last night. Four Muse Spark analysts check guest notes, plates, stock and the closing round, then Wonder links the facts. In the app, every line opens its evidence.

    Counts from a live run on the demo restaurant, Saffron House.

    1. Muse WonderReads the menu. Plans the morning review.
    2. Muse SparkNames the dish, writes the checks, runs four analysts.
    3. Muse ImageDraws plating cards, dish icons and table tents.
    4. Muse VoiceWrites down what chefs and guests say, in their language.
    5. GlimmerKeeps only frames with a plate, on site.
